# Utah course-planner research

**Checked:** 2026-09-25, for every source listed. Each fact carries a source key like [S1]. The full URL for each key is in the Sources table at the end.

**Scope:** high school graduation requirements, admission to Utah's public colleges and universities, state scholarships tied to high school coursework, and concurrent enrollment.

**Method:** everything below comes from primary sources: the Utah Administrative Code (via the eRules API), the Utah Code (le.utah.gov), enrolled bills, the Utah State Board of Education (USBE), the Utah System of Higher Education (USHE), and each institution's admissions pages. Items I could not verify are marked **UNVERIFIED**. Items that are changing are marked **CHANGING**.

---

## Key points for the planner

1. **Requirements depend on the graduating class.**
   - Students who were in grade 9 or 10 in 2026-27 (the classes of 2029 and 2030) and every later class need **3.5** social studies credits and **5.0** elective credits.
   - Students who were in grade 11 or 12 in 2026-27 (the classes of 2027 and 2028) need **3.0** social studies and **5.5** electives.
   - The total is **24 credits** either way. [S1][S3][S4]
2. **Utah math uses integrated course names.** The state rule names Secondary Mathematics I, II and III, not Algebra I, Geometry and Algebra II. [S1][S3]
3. **"Dual enrollment" means something else in Utah.** Rule R277-438 uses it for private or home-school students taking part-time courses or activities at a public school. College credit earned in high school is **concurrent enrollment (CE)**. The app's "dual enrollment" level should be labeled CE for Utah. [S50][S43]
4. **No basic civics test is required for a regular diploma.** HB 381 (2025) removed it as of 2025-07-01, and HB 312 (2026) repealed the statute completely as of 2026-07-01. Rule R277-700-8 has not caught up yet (see the flags section). [S12][S13][S1]
5. **Of the public universities, only Utah State publishes a recommended course pattern.** The University of Utah reviews applications holistically with an emphasis on rigor. Weber State, Utah Valley and Utah Tech are open admission. Southern Utah uses a GPA cutoff. The admissions pages checked for all six are test-optional. [S20][S25][S28][S31][S32][S34]
6. **The main state scholarship tied to coursework is the Opportunity Scholarship.** It requires a 3.3 GPA plus one AP, IB or CE course in each of language arts, math and science, taken in grades 9-12, and a completed FAFSA. [S36]

## 1. High school graduation requirements

The governing rule is **Utah Admin. Code R277-700-6** (last changed 2026-04-07; continuation notice 2026-03-16) [S1][S51]. The statute behind it is Utah Code **53E-4-204**, effective 2026-07-01 as amended by HB 312 (2026) [S7][S13].

### 1b. Social studies detail

**Classes of 2027 and 2028** (grades 11-12 in 2026-27) [S3]: 2.5 foundation credits plus 0.5 LEA-discretion credit.

| Course | Credits | Courses that qualify |
|---|---|---|
| World Geography | 0.5 | Geography for Life, World Geography, AP Human Geography, CE World/Cultural Geography |
| World History | 0.5 | World Civilization, World History, AP European History, AP World History, CE HIST 1100 + 1110 (both) |
| U.S. History 2 | 1.0 | U.S. History 2, AP US History, CE HIST 1700 (full year), CE HIST 2700 + 2710 (both) |
| U.S. Government & Citizenship | 0.5 | U.S. Government & Citizenship, AP US Gov & Politics, CE POLS 1100 |

**Class of 2029 and later** (grades 9-10 in 2026-27):
- **American Constitutional Government & Citizenship (ACGC)** replaces U.S. Government. It is **1.0 credit**, made of two distinct semesters that may not be repeated. [S1] 6(12); [S4]; [S7]; [S8]
- **U.S. History** must also be two distinct semesters. [S7] (HB 312, 2026)
- Up to 0.5 of the ACGC credit can come from a civics-focused verbal-communication course or activity, or from JROTC. That option applies only to the **second half of ACGC**. [S7]; [S4]
- Total: 3.0 foundation credits + 0.5 LEA-discretion = **3.5**. [S1]
- **CHANGING:**
  - ACGC is first offered in **2027-28**. Its standards are still being written with UVU's Civic Thought and Leadership Initiative. [S4][S8]
  - Prior U.S. Government & Citizenship credit **cannot** count toward ACGC. The old course is retired in 2027-28. [S4]
  - USBE says it is "not possible to determine" yet whether AP US Gov or CE political science will count. A one-semester CE course is "unlikely" to cover the 1.0. [S4]
  - Seniors in 2027-28 who lack U.S. Government take ACGC. Whether they may take only 0.5 of it is left to each LEA. [S4]
  - Beginning no later than 2027-28, ACGC must cover religion in U.S. history and religious liberty. [S8]

### 1c. Language arts and science course lists (USBE 2026-27) [S3]

**Language arts by level:**
- **Level 10:** ELA 10 or AP Seminar.
- **Level 11:** English 11, English 11 Honors, IB, AP Literature, or AP Language.
  - ENGL 1010 counts for level 11 **only** if taken before 2026-27 or through an approved pilot.
- **Level 12:** English 12, English CE, IB, AP Literature, or AP Language.
- **Applied/advanced list:** AP World Languages, Business Communication, Creative Writing 1-2, Debate 2-3, Humanities, Journalism 1-2, Literary Magazine, Literature, Mythology, Professional & Technical Communication, Speech 2.
- **CHANGING:** World Languages 3 and 4 **stop** counting as the 4th language arts credit beginning 2027-28. Credit taken earlier still counts.

**Advanced math** (Sec III is the prerequisite): AP Calculus AB/BC, AP Statistics, AP Precalculus, College Prep Math, CE 1010/1030/1040/1050/1060, IB, Introductory Calculus.

**Applied math that can replace Sec III** (Sec II is the prerequisite; must total 1.0):
- Accounting I and II (0.5 each)
- Computer Programming 1 (0.5, only if not used for digital studies or CTE)
- Computer Programming 2 (0.5)
- Introductory Statistics or Intro to Data Science (1.0)
- Mathematical Decision Making for Life (non-CE, 1.0)
- Mathematics of Personal Finance (1.0)
- Medical Math (0.5)
- Modern Mathematics (1.0)
- Small Business Math (0.5)

**Science foundation courses:**
- Biology: Biology, Biology: Agricultural Science, AP/IB Biology, CE Biology with lab.
- Chemistry: Chemistry, AP/IB Chemistry, CE Chemistry with lab.
- Computer Science: AP Computer Science, Computer Science Principles, Computer Science Advanced.
- Earth: Earth and Space Science, AP Environmental Science, IB Environmental Systems.
- Physics: Physics, AP/IB Physics, CE Physics with lab.
- A long applied/advanced list covers the third science credit, including many CTE agriculture, engineering and medical courses.
- **Flag:** the rule says "Computer Programming 2" where the 2026-27 list says "Computer Science Advanced", probably a renamed course. [S1] 6(11)(a)(v) vs [S3] p.4

### 1d. Credit before grade 9, middle school, and other rules

- **High school credit earned before grade 9 must be recognized** on the grades 9-12 transcript. This has been required since 2024-06-10. [S1] 6(3)(a); [S5]
  - High school courses may not replace middle school requirements. [S1] 6(3)(b)
  - USBE warns that pre-grade-9 credit goes into the grade 9 GPA and may affect scholarship, college or NCAA eligibility. [S5]
  - Health courses may not be taken outside the grades set in law. [S5]
- **Grades 7-8 General Core:**
  - ELA 7 and 8, Math 7 and 8, Integrated Science 7 and 8, U.S. History, Utah History.
  - At least one course each of Health, **College and Career Awareness**, **Digital Literacy**, the Arts, and PE.
  - LEAs may substitute a course with parent consent. [S1] 5
  - **CHANGING:** state standards for a grade 7/8 "digital skills course" (covering AI literacy, social media, privacy and similar topics) are due by 2027-28. [S10]
- **Math before grade 9:** a grade 7 or 8 student can earn high school credit for a foundation math course only if one of these applies:
  - the student is identified as gifted under R277-707;
  - the student is enrolled at both a middle/junior high and a high school;
  - the student has been promoted to grade 9;
  - the student passes the Board competency test the summer before grade 9.

  The student must still earn 3.0 more math credits in high school, including one from the approved list. [S1] 6(8)-(9)
- **Test-out options (CHANGING):** starting 2027-28, USBE must offer statewide test-outs for core classes in grades 9-12. Board rules for them are pending. [S9]
- **IEP and Plan for College and Career Readiness:** LEAs may modify graduation requirements through an IEP, or through the SEOP/PCCR with a parent signature. [S1] 6(24), 6(26)
- **College-and-career-ready math competency** (in addition to the 24 credits):
  - A college-bound student must meet **one** of: AP Calculus or AP Statistics score of 3 or higher; IB HL math 5 or higher; CLEP precalculus or calculus 50 or higher; **ACT math 26 or higher**; **SAT math 640 or higher**; or a C or better in a CE math course that meets the quantitative literacy requirement.
  - Otherwise, a college-bound senior who has not met one of these must **take a full year of math senior year**.
  - Non-college-bound students meet math competencies in their Plan for College and Career Readiness.
  - [S1] R277-700-9; statute [S11]
  - **Flag:** the qualifying scores are set by the Utah Board of Higher Education under 53E-4-206(4). I quoted the rule's numbers and did not check a separate USHE score table.
- **Statewide ACT:** districts must give the state's college-readiness assessment to **every grade 11 student**. USBE's page describes this as the statewide ACT for grade 11. [S14][S15]
- **Online courses (SOEP):** students in grades 6-12 at Utah public schools, and eligible home-based students, can take courses through the Statewide Online Education Program. This is a fallback when a school does not offer a course. [S16]

## 2. Admission to Utah public colleges and universities

**System level:** Utah has **16 public institutions**: 8 degree-granting and 8 technical colleges [S35][S17].

**Admit Utah** (launched 2024-10-04):
- Every Utah high school student is notified of guaranteed admission to one or more of the 16, "regardless of their GPA". Students must still apply to each school. [S18][S17]
- Applications to Utah public institutions are free for Utah residents. [S17]
- A direct data-integration pilot with Provo City School District launched 2025-11-25. [S19]
- **UNVERIFIED:** Admit Utah's GPA-to-institution mapping runs server-side and is not published.

**Major-level requirements:**
- At the U, "some majors require students to submit a separate application, go through an audition process, or meet other pre-requisites". Utah Direct does not guarantee admission to those majors. [S23][S22]
- At USU, applicants must meet a major's minimum GPA or may be offered an "Exploratory" major. [S25]
- The per-major rules were not collected in this pass.

**CE and freshman status:** at the U, college courses taken in high school do not change freshman status [S23]. The CE Handbook warns that more than 60 college credits may make a student ineligible for entering-student scholarships at some institutions [S43].

## 3. State scholarships tied to high school coursework

### Opportunity Scholarship

This is the statewide merit scholarship. Statute: Utah Code **53H-11-402**, effective 2026-07-01 as amended by 2026 Ch. 138. The Board of Higher Education sets the academic standards by rule. [S37][S36]

**Requirements (current USHE page, same for the classes of 2026 and 2027):** [S36]
- A **3.3 cumulative high school GPA**. **Flag:** the page does not say whether this is weighted or unweighted.
- **Three advanced courses:** one AP, IB or CE course in each of **math, science and language arts**. The course must map to a USBE core code starting **06** (language arts), **07** (math) or **08** (science).
- The courses must be completed in **grades 9-12**, before graduation.
- USHE warns: "scholarship course requirements may not align with graduation requirements."
- **For CE science, lecture credit is required and lab credit is optional.** A lecture-only CE course may **not** earn the foundation science credit for graduation, which usually needs lecture plus lab.
- Math 1010 counts for the scholarship but does not meet the general-education quantitative literacy requirement.
- A completed FAFSA, and graduation from a Utah high school. Private high school students also submit a transcript.
- **Eligible-course lists:** examples by subject and the CE Master List. [S36][S48]

**Award:**
- The class of 2026 could receive up to **$800 per semester for four semesters ($3,200)**.
- It covers tuition and general fees after other state aid.
- It requires full-time enrollment the fall after graduation, unless the student has an approved deferment (military, medical, service, or a Utah technical college).
- It can be used at the 8 public degree-granting schools plus BYU (Provo), Ensign, WGU and Westminster. [S36]
- The statute says the maximum amount is set each year based on funding and the number of eligible students. [S37]

**Class of 2027 dates:** the application opens 2027-01-04; priority deadline 2027-01-29; final deadline 2027-03-18; decisions by 2027-05-07. **CHANGING:** the class of 2027 award amount has not been announced. [S36]

**The "Regents' Scholarship" is not in current code.** The Part 4 scholarship index in Title 53H lists "Opportunity" and no Regents' program. [S49] Utah's higher-education code has been renumbered from Title 53B to **Title 53H** [S49].

### First Credential Scholarship (formerly PRIME)

This program was created by HB 260 (2025) and took effect 2025-07-01. [S40][S41]

**Class of 2026 requirements:** a 2026-27 FAFSA, plus **one** of the following:
- **five general-education CE courses, each in a different GE category**;
- **six courses or 300 hours of CTE at a Utah technical college**;
- **300 hours of an approved youth apprenticeship**.

The award is a one-time **$625** (subject to funding). [S40]

**CHANGING, class of 2027 and later:** eligibility will follow the First Credential Master Plan, and USHE says details are "coming". The USBE Master Plan defines five routes: [S41]
- **CE route:** 12-18 core GE credits across categories, or a UBHE-prescribed set, that must include one quantitative literacy course (MATH 1030, 1040 or 1050) and one writing course (ENGL 1010, 2010 or 2015).
- An industry-recognized credential.
- A youth apprenticeship (300 hours or more).
- A technical college certificate (or at least 300 hours toward one).
- A completed state-approved CTE program of study.

### New Century Scholarship

It is **closed**: the Board "may not accept a new application … on or after August 15, 2021". A student cannot hold both New Century and Opportunity. Do not suggest it. [S38]

### State aid not tied to coursework (for context)

- **Utah Promise Grant:** need-based. Requires residency, a FAFSA and demonstrated need. Covers up to the cost of attendance until the first bachelor's degree or 120 attempted credits. [S39]
- Also on the USHE list: T.H. Bell (future educators), Karen Mayne Public Safety Officer, Technical Education, Talent Development, NASA Space Grant. [S35]

### Institution scholarships that reward rigor

- **USU Advanced Coursework Scholarship:** for Utah residents entering as first-year students. A two-year award for "AP, Concurrent Enrollment, Honors/Extended, IB, or Tech Ed". Requires at least a 3.0 GPA and stacks with GPA-based awards. For students without test scores, USU "automatically consider[s] … based on the 'rigor'" of their coursework. [S27][S26]
- **U of U "For Utah":** covers four years of tuition and fees for Pell-eligible Utah residents with a high school GPA of **3.2 or higher**. Apply by Dec 1; FAFSA by Feb 1. [S24]
- **SUU:** awards by unweighted GPA **or** ACT/SAT. Example: 3.40-3.59 GPA, or 23-26 ACT, or 1130-1250 SAT is the first resident tier. [S33]

## 4. Concurrent enrollment (CE), Utah's college credit in high school

**Legal basis:**
- Statute: Utah Code 53E-10-301 through 305. [S45][S46][S47]
- USBE rule R277-701, Early College Programs (changed 2025-11-21). [S44]
- UBHE policy R765-165, summarized in the 2025 CE Handbook. [S43]

**Who can enroll:** [S45]
- Public school students in **grades 9-12** who have a Plan for College and Career Readiness on file.
- Not home-schooled students (unless enrolled in a public school), not foreign exchange students, and not fifth-year students. [S43]

**Academic criteria:** [S43]
- Set jointly by the LEA and the institution, and "sufficiently selective to predict a successful experience". [S44] R277-701-7
- Typically "a B average or ACT score of 22 or higher", or a placement score. The FAQ mentions indicators such as a 3.0 GPA. [S43]
- **CE math requires Secondary Math I, II and III with a C average or better.** [S43]

**Enrollment protections:**
- A school "may not require prerequisites" for AP, IB or CE beyond USHE policy.
- A school may not block enrollment because of grades, test scores, grade level, lack of a teacher referral, or honors prerequisites. [S44] R277-701-6

**Allowed courses:**
- Statute: general education, CTE, pre-major, foreign language, or Board-approved upper-division courses, at the 1000 level or above. [S46]
- Rule: English, math, fine arts, humanities, science, social science, world languages and CTE. [S44] R277-701-8
- Courses must be on the **CE Master List**, which can be downloaded by year and filtered by district. [S48]

**Limits:** [S43][S44]
- Up to **30 contractual CE credits per school year**. Credits beyond that are at the student's cost.
- **No state-funded repeats** after credit, a failing grade, or an instructor withdrawal.
- A "W" can be retaken with approval.

**Grades:**
- Grades go on **both** the high school transcript (they count in the high school GPA) and a permanent college transcript. [S43]
- A C- or lower may not count at the college. [S43]

**Cost:**
- Statutory cap of **$30 per credit hour**, lowered to $5 for free/reduced-lunch students, $10 when a high school teacher teaches the course, and $15 for video delivery. [S47]
- Institutions were charging **$5 per credit hour in 2025-26**. [S43]
- LEAs must pay CE fees for students with socioeconomic disadvantage. Textbooks, lab fees and similar costs follow fee-waiver rules. [S43][S44]

**Transfer:** CE credit transfers among USHE institutions. It may come in as an equivalent course or as an elective. [S43][S44]

**Advising tools:**
- USHE general-education "navigators" for each institution. [S42]
- The Utah Majors Guide (utahmajors.org) and the Utah Transfer Guide. [S42]

**"Early College" means two things:**
- For **USBE**, it covers AP, IB and CE together.
- For **USHE**, it means students paying full tuition on a college campus; that credit is **not** automatically on the high school transcript. [S43]

**Associate degree:** a student can finish one in high school; USHE recommends planning from grade 9. [S43]

**CE and graduation credit:**
- CE English counts for level 12 language arts.
- CE Biology, Chemistry or Physics *with lab* counts as foundation science.
- CE math 1010/1030/1040/1050/1060 counts as advanced math.
- CE HIST or POLS can meet specific social studies requirements.
- [S3]

## Flags: uncertain or changing

1. **R277-700-8 is out of date.** It still requires a basic civics test for adult-education diplomas. HB 312 (2026) repealed that statute (53E-4-205) as of 2026-07-01, and HB 381 (2025) had already removed the test for regular diplomas. Expect a rule amendment. [S1][S12][S13]
2. **The rule text and the USBE course list disagree on electives.** R277-700-6(20) says electives are 5.0, but the 24.0 total only works with 3.5 social studies. USBE's list says **5.5 for the classes of 2027 and 2028**. Use the USBE list. [S1][S3]
3. **ACGC:** standards are being written, the course starts 2027-28, and which AP or CE courses will count is undetermined. [S4][S8]
4. **Arts credit:** LEAs may count applied crafts and technical arts courses (HB 312, 2026). USBE says guidance is pending, so arts credit will vary by LEA. [S3][S13]
5. **Changes arriving in 2027-28:**
   - Finance CE stops counting for financial literacy.
   - World Languages 3 and 4 stop counting as the 4th language arts credit.
   - Statewide core-class test-outs begin.
   - Grade 7/8 digital skills standards arrive.
   - [S3][S9][S10]
6. **First Credential rules for the class of 2027 and later** are not published yet. [S40][S41]
7. **Opportunity Scholarship:** the class of 2027 award amount is unknown, and the page does not say whether the 3.3 GPA is weighted or unweighted. [S36]
8. **Snow College and SLCC admission policies** were not verified.
9. **Admit Utah's GPA-to-institution mapping** is not published.
10. **"Secondary Math IE/IIE/IIIE" and "Extended":** USU groups "Honors/Extended" together as rigorous coursework, which suggests "E" means an honors-type track. I found no definition from USBE, so treat this as uncertain. [S3][S27]
11. **Computer science foundation course names** differ between the rule ("Computer Programming 2") and the USBE list ("Computer Science Advanced"). [S1][S3]
12. **R277-438 (Dual Enrollment)** shows an eRules effective end date of 2026-10-07; its last continuation notice was 2021-10-07. This does not affect CE. [S50]

## Notes for implementation

- Store Utah rule sets by graduating class: 2027-2028 versus 2029 and later.
- Use the USBE "Courses Meeting the Criteria" PDF for each year as the source for which courses meet each requirement. [S3]
- Load the CE Master List (downloadable by year) as reference data. Its core codes (06/07/08) let the planner check Opportunity Scholarship eligibility directly. [S48]
- If a school's course guide lists USBE CACTUS or core codes (11-digit course codes, per R277-701-9), the extraction step can map courses exactly.
- In the planner:
  - Add a senior-year math prompt for college-bound students who have not met the math competency.
  - Show a warning when a CE science course is lecture-only.
  - Show the pre-grade-9 credit caveats.
